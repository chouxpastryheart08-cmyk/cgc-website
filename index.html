Our Story

From backyard beds to your bath.

Couture Garden Collective started with a simple idea: the best ingredients are the ones you grow yourself. What began as handmade soap and bath products in 2020 has grown into a working urban farm in South Chicagoland.

Today our raised beds hold lavender, chamomile, roses, echinacea, marigolds, and kitchen herbs. We harvest, dry, and infuse them right here, then pour them into small batches of soap, mist, butter, and candles.

Every bar is cured, cut, and wrapped by hand. No shortcuts, no mass production, just plants, patience, and care.
